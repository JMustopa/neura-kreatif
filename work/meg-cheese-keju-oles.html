MEG Cheese Keju Oles.

Big idea: The Smart Upgrade. Redefining everyday cheese with the right balance of quality, versatility, and value — a smarter solution for fillings and toppings, for food businesses and culinary enthusiasts alike.

Client: MEG CheeseProject: Digital Campaign Launch 2026Type: Ad hocServices: KOL · KOC · Paid Media

What we did.

The truth

An everyday ingredient, upgraded.

Cheese is bought on habit. The opportunity was to make people rethink a basic ingredient as an everyday essential — value without sacrificing quality, aimed at Gen-Z and newlywed families.

KOL

Curated voices, real fit.

Our KOL service connects brands with carefully curated influencers who align with the brand’s character and target audience — from talent selection and content planning to campaign reporting, so every collaboration delivers real impact on awareness and sales.

KOC

Honest reviews from real consumers.

Our KOC (Key Opinion Consumer) program connects the brand with real consumers who share genuine, honest reviews. Relatable, relevant content that builds trust, boosts awareness, and drives conversions.

Paid media

Targeting, always-on, frequency.

Audience targeting by behavior, interest and location; an always-on presence across Instagram and TikTok; and frequency that re-engages people who already know the brand — until they purchase.
